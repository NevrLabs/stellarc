## Headline

**shadcn's trunk broke a five-window dependabot-only streak — and the fix that did it lands directly on our transcript lane.** [`#11465`](https://github.com/shadcn-ui/ui/pull/11465) (merged 11-02) fixes the official `message-scroller-streaming` chat primitive: **a manual scroll during an anchored streaming turn destroyed the reserved tail spacer.** With `autoScroll` on, follow-mode re-armed unconditionally, zeroed the spacer in one frame and *yanked the viewport to the live edge even while the reply was still small*; with `autoScroll` off, nothing re-armed and a viewport of dead space stayed stranded below the reply forever. Root cause: the re-arm branch in `reconcileFollowMode` had no spacer condition, and `scrollable.end` measures against `contentBottom`, which excludes the spacer — so a reader sitting inside the spacer zone looked "at the end" and re-armed anyway. PR #91 (NEVR-170) installs exactly these primitives as our agent-transcript foundation, and our transcript streams token-by-token with an anchored scroller — a reader scrolling while an agent reply streams is our *primary* interaction. Spec-delta amendment in proposal 1.

**@shadcn/lint shipped 0.2.4 this morning — a hostile-input and supply-chain hardening drop on the exact linter our T02-relief TRIAL brief pins.** 0.2.3 → **0.2.4** (npm 07:34Z, release 07:29Z, repo `shadcn-ui/lint`): **read hostile class tokens in linear time** (#152 — ReDoS-class fix), **cap the depth of package-exports conditions** (#154), **treat a const chain past 64 links as dynamic** (#161 — bounded analysis), **stop resolving theme variables that fan out** (#153), and **keep package-exports/imports targets inside the package** (#155 — no traversal outside the analyzed package). Plus release-train hardening: signed commits required on PRs (#158), pinned actions + publish from a changesets-only job (#156). For us this is the difference between "lint our own tokens" and "lint whatever an agent or adversarial input produces" — the 0.2.3 pin in the filed TRIAL brief moves to 0.2.4 (proposal 2). ## Proposals

### 1. ADOPT (amendment to the NEVR-170 / PR #91 brief) — pin the anchored-stream scroll contract to the post-#11465 behavior

No new issue: extend the filed NEVR-170 brief. The transcript scroller built on the chat primitives must satisfy the contract #11465 just fixed upstream, stated as testable laws:

1. A **manual scroll during an anchored streaming turn releases the anchor** — the reader keeps their position; nothing re-arms follow until they return to the live edge.
2. **The reserved tail spacer drains when the anchor releases** — no one-frame collapse that yanks the viewport to the live edge (the `autoScroll`-on bug), and no dead space stranded below the reply (the `autoScroll`-off bug).
3. **"At the end" is measured including the spacer zone** — a reader sitting inside the reserved room is *not* at the end and must not re-arm follow (`scrollable.end` vs `contentBottom`).
4. When #91 rebases on post-#11465 upstream, add a regression play for each law (stream a reply, scroll mid-stream, assert position held + spacer drained; scroll to true bottom, assert follow re-armed).

Cite: shadcn-ui/ui#11465 (merged 2026-11-02, fixes #11125) — the upstream fix and its root-cause write-up are the spec.

### 2. ADOPT (amendment to the @shadcn/lint TRIAL brief) — pin 0.2.4 and cite the hostile-input hardening

No new issue: extend the 10-24 TRIAL brief. (a) Pin moves 0.2.3 → **0.2.4** (published 2026-11-02). (b) New cites for the brief body: hostile class tokens are read in **linear time** (#152) and analysis is bounded (exports-condition depth capped #154; const chains past 64 links treated as dynamic #161; theme-variable fan-out stopped #153) — the linter is now safe to run over large token chains and adversarial input, which is the agent-written-CSS profile we would actually lint. (c) Supply-chain cites: PRs require signed commits (#158), CI actions pinned, publishes come from a changesets-only job (#156) — the release train itself is hardened. The rest of the brief (dedicated Oxlint/ESLint pass scoped to `apps/stellarc-ui`, devDependency + one CI step, no runtime deps) stands.
