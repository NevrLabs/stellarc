### 2. WATCH (pre-registered) — the capability-declaration law for embedded clients

NSH v0.6.0's #1499 makes it release-grade: **an app that embeds a runtime declares capabilities for application-level powers (main window, app menu, tray, global shortcuts, quit/restart, launch at login) and for home-directory access — and an undeclared power is refused, not defaulted on** ("closes a sandbox escape for embedded apps"). Paired with #1709/#2135 (WebSocket/updater/SVG/HTTP/WebView2/media become optional features — the embedding law as a build property), this is the two-sided contract ADR 0015's clients/arclet lane needs: *pay only for what you declare, get only what you declare*. Pre-registered to fold into the ADR 0015 step-2+ briefs (the review-input row is the host); nothing to file until an embedding surface is chartered in Stellarc.
