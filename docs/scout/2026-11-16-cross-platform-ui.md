## Proposals

### 1. WATCH (fold-by-pattern) — the scroll-honesty family grows by two

**Rune #1054** (at-bottom scroll reports confirm existing state without rebuilding the view; the conditional callbacks *return whether the view changed*) and **NSH #2159** (a `scrollTo` whose target the content has not reached yet is held until it does) extend the §A anchor/scroll family with its report-side half: *scroll-position reporting and programmatic scrolling are reads that must not mutate what the reader sees — a report confirms state, and an intent that cannot be satisfied yet waits rather than lands wrong.* Fold as an addendum to the agent-view contracts §A with the next contracts addendum (design lane owns the vehicle; row 58 of the INDEX is the host). Same fold: gpui-kit #3922 `scroll_to_offset` already carries the restore half.

### 2. WATCH (pre-registered) — a reduced-motion law for apps/web

gpui-kit v0.10.0 ships **an explicit reduced-motion preference** (#3881) — the second native shell this month to treat reduced motion as a first-class preference rather than a media-query afterthought. Pre-register for apps/web, to fire before the first animated agent surface (apps/web has no motion dep today — the design lane's standing decision): *every animation Stellarc ships respects an explicit reduced-motion preference (user-agent `prefers-reduced-motion` **and** an in-app setting where the OS signal is unavailable), and every motion has a non-animated terminal state that conveys the same information.* This is an acceptance line for future work, not a brief — nothing to file until a motion dep exists.
