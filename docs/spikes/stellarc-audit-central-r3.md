### Fail-open assessment

`error_mode: ignore` is **not fail-closed**: an evaluation error makes the drop condition ineffective and can retain a record that should drop. The separate canary prepends `ParseJSON(body)["x"] == true` to the first predicate; it parses successfully but fails evaluating ordinary bodies. All 43 are retained and the gate fails. Ten warning lines do not mean only ten failures: collector diagnostics may be sampled. This canary is not a proposed patch and must never be deployed.

No fail-open/type-error was observed in the unchanged proposed clause across the 43 original cases and 13 additional probes. Its typed probes are bounded evidence, not a proof over every possible OTLP input or future processor version. IsMatch/Coalesce on tested non-string IDs did not throw; IsString(body) protects the kind match in the tested paths. Do not casually broaden the clause or change error_mode globally: either can alter unrelated deployed policy. Future predicate edits require the same error canary, exact-set and typed gates. Existing legacy conditions may have other malformed-type behavior; this report does not certify the entire collector as fail-closed.

## Security corrections to R2

1. **UUID shape is not meaningful forgery cost.** A sender can cheaply generate a matching string. The regex is format validation, not an installation allowlist or authenticated sender binding; it does not even require a particular installation UUID.
2. **Client labels are not identity.** Service, scope, body kind and event ID are sender-controlled. A fully matching forged row is indistinguishable here. Existing WARN spoofability demonstrates another retention path, not verified Stellarc source attribution or an argument that attribution is unnecessary. If audit authenticity is required, collector/ingest owners must bind trusted sender identity at an authenticated boundary and prevent client override; that is a separate design/implementation gate, not achieved by this patch.
3. **1000/org outbox capacity is not a rate bound.** Draining permits unlimited continued production over time; org count and sender direct submissions are additional dimensions. No sustained rate, warehouse-volume, storage-cost or denial-of-service bound was measured or established. R2's “no meaningful warehouse-volume risk” is unsupported.
4. **Retention, authenticity and capacity are distinct.** This candidate passes a bounded retention-behavior gate only. Keep security source-binding and volume controls explicit open requirements where required; do not close them from label conjunctions or existing WARN behavior.
