What changed from your feedback

  • One-row header, identity in the header. Org, tabs, search, your avatar, window controls. Tabs carry the icon of what is open, not avatars.
  • Tabs can't eat the search bar. Every header item is fixed size except the tab strip. Tabs are 200px, shrink evenly to 88px, then the strip clips and a count button shows how many are hidden. The active tab always scrolls into view. Measured at 1024, 1280 and 1440px with 4, 12 and 20 tabs: search stays 232px and unmoved. Try "Stress tabs" in the review bar.
  • Linear-style board views. Board, List, Timeline, Backlog, Calendar as defaults, then saved custom views (Agent work, Mine this week) and "+ View" to save the current filter. Filter and Display sit at the right of the same row. The tab title follows the view ("Engineering, Timeline").
  • Recent pinned to the sidebar footer in every view, with Ctrl+E. Click opens it in this tab; Ctrl+click opens a new tab. Inbox stays in Home.
  • Polish: 4 rounds of vision critique checked against DOM measurements. Fixed a CSS collision that squashed the New ticket button, made avatars one language (square tile; tinted initial for agents, dashed for unassigned), made priority icons legible, sorted list rows by due date, gave the calendar a sixth week, and made timeline bars show dates or live status instead of repeating the title.
